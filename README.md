# BWA-Finance-Performance-Overview-2020-2025
## Overview
This project presents an interactive finance dashboard for BWA Tech, designed to support executive‑level decision making and the company’s strategic expansion into Latin America (LATAM).
The dashboard consolidates multi‑year financial data (2020–2025) to provide a single source of truth for Net Sales, Gross Margin, and Net Profit, with drill‑downs by market and division.

## Business context and problem statement
BWA Tech aims to accelerate growth in Latin America but historically relied on fragmented financial reports and manual spreadsheets.
These manual processes slowed down decision making and increased the risk of data manipulation or inconsistencies, making it difficult for leadership to assess market performance—especially in key countries like Brazil.

## Problem statement

BWA Tech seeks to expand in LATAM but lacks a reliable, real‑time financial view to guide this strategy.
The company needs a finance dashboard that delivers trusted, actionable insights by market and division, enabling faster and more objective decisions for the region.

Data scope: Financial data for 2020–2025, aggregated at monthly or quarterly level, with breakdowns by market and division.

Data model: Fact tables for financial transactions or aggregated P&L lines. Dimension tables for time, market (country/region), and division/product.

Key metrics: Net Sales, Gross Margin, Gross Margin %, Operating Expenses, Net Profit, Net Profit %, LY, YOY, YOY %.

Visualization: Power BI

Presentation: PowerPoint

Insights and impact
The business shows strong Net Sales and Net Profit growth over the 2020–2025 period, though Gross Margin does not fully meet target, signalling cost or pricing pressures.

Brazil underperforms relative to its potential, which is critical because it should act as a growth engine for LATAM; this gap directly challenges the company’s regional expansion goals.
Certain divisions (e.g., Networking or other key categories) emerge as profit engines, while others require margin improvement or portfolio review.

The dashboard enables executives to quickly see where performance supports the LATAM strategy and where corrective actions are needed (pricing, cost management, market focus, or product mix).
